Step 1: The Winning Formula

The Golden 4-Paragraph Template

Paragraph 1 · Introduction ~18–22 words

State your opinion clearly (do not copy the question word-for-word), then say you have two reasons.

I think that [topic is good / bad].

I have two reasons for this.

Or: "I do not think that..."
Paragraph 2 · Reason 1 + Example ~25–30 words

Use POINT #1 in your first sentence. Add a second sentence with an example or explanation.

First, [Reason 1 using Point #1].

For example, [specific detail or result].

Detail starter: "For example," / "This is because"
Paragraph 3 · Reason 2 + Detail ~25–30 words

Use POINT #2 in your first sentence. Follow up with why this matters.

Second, [Reason 2 using Point #2].

This is because [explanation / result].

Or: "In addition," / "As a result,"
Paragraph 4 · Conclusion ~15–18 words

Repeat your opinion using slightly different words (e.g., change I think to I believe or I disagree).

For these reasons, I believe that [restate opinion].

Keep it 1 strong sentence!

Essay #2 Passing Model Revision

94 Words (Target Hit!)

Intro I disagree with this idea. I have two reasons for this.

Reason 1 First of all, recently we have many ways to use social media, like Instagram and TikTok. However, these apps can sometimes be dangerous because young people might get involved in crimes.

Reason 2 Second of all, using smartphones too much might cause neck injuries and a lack of studying. For example, students often stay up late watching videos instead of doing homework.

Conclusion Therefore, I think that junior high school students having their own smartphones is not a good idea.

Intro I do not think that online shopping is better than shopping at physical stores. I have two reasons for this.

Reason 1 First, online shops do not have the fun of shopping. At physical stores, people can walk around and see new items in person.

Reason 2 Second, when you shop at an online store, it is easy to lose track of what you bought. You might order too many things without noticing.

Conclusion For these reasons, I disagree with the idea that online shopping is better than physical shopping.

Step 4: Your Personal Watchlist

Top 6 Trap Points & Personal Spelling Checklist

Every fix below comes directly from your Essay #1 and Essay #2 drafts. Review these 6 cards in the final 3 minutes of the exam!

1

Use "disagree with" (Not "to")

Never write "I disagree to...". Memorize these two safe opening & closing patterns:

I disagree to this idea.
✓ I disagree with this idea.
✓ I do not think that [topic]...
2

Homophones & Word Spacing

Watch out for words that sound identical or should be written as one single word:

two reasonsto reasons
sometimessome times
Therefore,there for
smartphonessmart phones
3

"because" vs. "because of"

When a full clause (Subject + Verb) follows, always use because without of:

because of you might get into...
✓ because you might get into...
("because of" only takes a noun: "because of the rain")
4

Don't Put "By" Before a Subject

An -ing verb can act as the subject of a sentence on its own—drop the word by:

By using smartphones might cause...
✓ Using smartphones too much might cause...
5

Your Personal Spelling Watchlist

Double-check -y endings (keep y before -ing; change y to ies for plurals):

studying(keep the y!)
injuries(not injurys)
crimes(not craims)
shopping / physical(pp / single l)
6

Avoid Japanese-English ("SNS")

"SNS" is Japanese-English (Wasei-eigo) and isn't understood in Western English. Always write social media and capitalize app names:

use sns, like instagram and tictok
✓ use social media, like Instagram and TikTok
