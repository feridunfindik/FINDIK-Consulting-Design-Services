How NewsQuest makes money

Four revenue lines, in the order they switch on.

01

Freemium → Pro

Subscriptions from power users and exam-season families. The core habit stays free.

02

B2B Education

Per-student licences for schools and 塾, sold on the teacher dashboard and printable worksheets.

03

Rewarded ads (Free)

Optional: watch a short ad to unlock a bonus game or a streak shield. Never mid-quiz, never for kids' classes.

04

Sponsored quests

Museums, publishers and exam boards sponsor clearly-labelled topic quests — e.g. a science-museum “Space Week”.

Family plan

¥980/month covers 1 parent + up to 3 children on Pro, with one combined weekly report — the natural upsell from how NewsQuest started: one parent, one student, one daily quiz.
